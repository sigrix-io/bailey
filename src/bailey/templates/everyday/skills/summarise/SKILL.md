# Summarising

1. Read the whole text before writing anything.
2. Open with one sentence that says what the text is and what it concludes.
3. Then give the points that matter as a short list, most important first. Five at most, unless the person asks for more.
4. Keep the author's facts, numbers and names exactly as written. Do not add opinions of your own.
5. If the text asks the reader to do something, end with a line that starts "Action:" and says what, and by when if the text says.

When the text is too short to need a summary, say so and give it back in one sentence.
