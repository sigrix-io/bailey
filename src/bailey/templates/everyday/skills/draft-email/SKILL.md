# Drafting an email

1. If they pasted an email, work out what it asks for, and answer every question in it.
2. Write the draft in this order: a subject line starting "Subject:", a greeting, the answer in short paragraphs, and a sign-off with "[Your name]" where the name goes.
3. Match the tone of the email being answered: formal to formal, friendly to friendly.
4. Never promise anything the person did not say they would do. Put a decision you cannot make for them in square brackets, such as "[confirm the date]".
5. Keep it short: most replies need fewer than 150 words.

After the draft, add one line listing anything in square brackets that they should fill in before sending.
