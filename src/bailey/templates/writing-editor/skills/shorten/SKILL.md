# Shortening

1. Aim for about two thirds of the original length, unless the person names a length.
2. Cut repetition, filler words and anything that restates the point; keep every fact, name and number.
3. Prefer short sentences and plain words, but keep the person's voice.
4. After the shorter text, give the word counts before and after, and name anything you cut that carried meaning.
